Welcome

The Night Sky Animal Shelter helps dogs find loving homes and helps lost dogs get back to their families. On this site you can meet dogs available for adoption, see dogs we have found, learn the adoption process, check our hours, and send us an inquiry.

Every dog deserves a warm place to sleep under the stars.

Original motto of the fictional Night Sky Animal Shelter

Adoption Process

These steps describe how adoption would work at our fictional shelter.

  1. Submit an application. Tell us about your home, your schedule, and your experience with dogs.
  2. Talk with an adoption counselor. We will review your application and answer your questions.
  3. Meet your dog. Visit the shelter and spend time with the dog you are interested in.
  4. Meet the family. If you have other pets or children, bring them to make sure everyone gets along.
  5. Complete the adoption. Sign the adoption agreement and pay the adoption fee.
  6. Take your dog home. We will give you care instructions and a follow-up contact.
