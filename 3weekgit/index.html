Adoption Process

Here is the sample adoption process for this fictional shelter.

  1. Application: Complete an adoption application with details about your home and lifestyle.
  2. Meeting: Meet the dog in person so you can learn if you are a good match.
  3. Adoption Steps: Finish the paperwork, ask questions, and review adoption requirements.
  4. Bring Your Dog Home: After approval, take your dog home and start your new life together.
