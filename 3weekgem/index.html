Welcome to The Night Sky Animal Shelter

At The Night Sky Animal Shelter, we are dedicated to rescuing stray, abandoned, and surrendered dogs and helping them find safe, lifelong homes. Whether you are looking to welcome a new furry companion into your family or searching for a lost pet, our staff and volunteers are here to help.

Here on our website, you can view dogs currently up for adoption, report or search for found dogs, read about our adoption requirements, and send us an inquiry.

Like stars in the night sky, every dog deserves a chance to shine in a safe, loving home.

Adoption Process

Our shelter uses a structured adoption procedure to ensure every dog is matched with a suitable, long-term household.

  1. Submit an Application: Fill out an adoption inquiry form online or visit the shelter in person to complete a questionnaire.
  2. Application Review: Our care team reviews your living arrangement, household needs, and pet history within 2–3 business days.
  3. Meet and Greet: Schedule an in-person meeting at the shelter to interact with the dog and ensure good compatibility.
  4. Home Agreement & Finalization: Complete the adoption contract, pay the sample adoption fee, and take your new companion home!
