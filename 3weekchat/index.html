Adoption Process

This is an example of how an adoption process might work at a shelter.

  1. Submit an application. Share information about your home, schedule, and experience with pets.
  2. Meet the dog. Arrange a visit to get to know the dog and ask questions.
  3. Review the details. Discuss care needs, responsibilities, and any adoption requirements.
  4. Complete the adoption. If approved, finish the paperwork and prepare to welcome your dog home.
